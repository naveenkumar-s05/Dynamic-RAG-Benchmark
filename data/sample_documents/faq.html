Northwind Frequently Asked Questions

What is the maximum file size?

The maximum single file size depends on your plan: 50 GB for Basic and Standard, and 500 GB for Enterprise.

Does Northwind support Linux?

Yes. Native agents are available for Windows, macOS and Linux distributions. The Linux agent is distributed as a deb or rpm package.

How do I contact support?

Open a ticket in the customer portal or email support@northwind.example. Enterprise customers also have a 24/7 phone line.

Where are backups stored?

Backups are stored in the region you select when creating the account. Data never leaves that region unless you enable cross-region replication.
